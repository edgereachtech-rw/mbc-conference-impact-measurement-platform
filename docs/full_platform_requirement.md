**M.BRYAN CONFERENCE IMPACT MEASUREMENT PLATFORM**

**Development Workplan and Requirements**

**First Pilot: ITTEC 2026**

**Prepared for:** M.Bryan Consulting Limited  
**Conference dates:** November 12–13, 2026  
**Conference format:** Physical and virtual  
**Platform ready for use:** **No later than November 7, 2026**

**1\. Purpose of the Platform**

M.Bryan Consulting Limited is committed to helping organisations demonstrate the value of their training investments. As the Convener of ITTEC, M.Bryan will apply the same principles to measuring and evaluating its own conference.

The platform will enable M.Bryan to follow each delegate’s journey:

**Expectations and baseline → Conference experience and learning → Transfer planning → Workplace application → Results and expectations.**

The platform will also support delegates to plan and apply what they learn, receive reminders according to their preferences, record their progress and access their own information.

ITTEC 2026 will be the first conference to use the platform. The platform should be designed for future use with other M.Bryan conferences, webinars, workshops and learning programmes.

**2\. Questions the Platform Must Help M.Bryan Answer**

1. Who attended ITTEC, and what did they expect?

2. What challenges and current practices did delegates identify before the conference?

3. What did delegates learn and experience during the conference?

4. What do delegates intend to apply?

5. How many delegates completed Individual Transfer Plans?

6. What have delegates applied after the conference?

7. What supported or prevented application?

8. What changes and results have delegates reported or evidenced?

9. To what extent were delegate and nominating organisation expectations met?

10. What should M.Bryan improve in future conferences?

**4\. The ITTEC 2026 Measurement Journey**

| Stage | Purpose | Delegate activity | Information captured |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Before ITTEC** | Establish expectations and baseline. | Sign up and complete the pre-conference assessment. | Expectations, challenges, knowledge, confidence and current practices. |
| **Day 1** | Capture learning and the delegate experience. | Complete a brief Day 1 check-in. | Learning, intended application and issues requiring attention. |
| **Day 2** | Capture learning from the day. | Complete a separate Day 2 check-in. | Day 2 learning and reflections. |
| **Closing Transfer Planning session** | Help delegates plan applications. | Complete an Individual Transfer Plan during the dedicated 30-minute session. | Planned actions, timelines, support needs and expected results. |
| **End of conference** | Evaluate the complete conference experience and learning. | Complete the final conference evaluation and learning assessment. And set reminder preferences | Experience, learning, expectations and readiness to apply. |
| **Approximately 30 days after** | Establish whether application has begun. | Complete a follow-up linked to the delegate’s plan. | First actions, progress, barriers and support needs. |
| **Approximately 60 days after** | Assess application and changes in practice. | Complete the second follow-up. | Application, changes, evidence and support. |
| **Approximately 90 days after** | Assess progress and emerging results. | Complete the third follow-up. | Progress, expectations, available results and evidence. |
| **Approximately 180 days or later, where relevant** | Capture outcomes that need more time. | Selected delegates complete a further follow-up. | Longer-term application and results. |

The platform must allow M.Bryan to adjust follow-up dates where needed.

**Reminder preferences and evaluation follow-ups are related but different:** delegates choose when and how often they receive reminders to support their own actions. The conference evaluation follow-ups are scheduled by M.Bryan to collect evidence at the stages above. Delegates must be informed about both.

**9\. Transfer Planning Sessions**

**9.1 Purpose**

ITTEC has deliberately included Transfer Planning sessions on **Day 1 and Day 2** to help delegates turn their conference learning into workplace action.

* **Day 2:** Delegates return to the same plans, add or refine their ideas based on their Day 2 learning, identify their priorities and complete their plans.

The completed plans will support application after ITTEC and provide the basis for delegates’ chosen reminders and post-conference follow-up.

**11.6 Application status: definitions**

Each action in the Individual Transfer Plan must have one current status.

| Status | Definition | Example |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Not yet started** | The delegate has not begun the planned action. | The delegate plans to meet a line manager but has not scheduled or held the meeting. |
| **Started** | The delegate has taken an initial step, but the planned action is not yet sufficiently advanced to be considered in progress. | The delegate has contacted the line manager to arrange a meeting. |
| **In progress** | The delegate is actively working on the action, but it has not yet reached the planned point of completion or application. | The delegate is developing an application-tracking process with relevant stakeholders. |
| **Applied** | The delegate has used the learning or practice at least once in a real work situation. | The delegate used the new tracking process for one training programme. |
| **Applied consistently** | The delegate reports using the learning or practice repeatedly over the period they describe. | The delegate has used the tracking process across several training programmes. |
| **Completed** | The planned action has been finished according to the delegate’s stated goal. | The delegate’s planned task was to introduce a tracking process, and it has been introduced. |
| **Changed** | The original action has been revised because the delegate’s circumstances, priorities or approach changed. | The delegate changes the planned initiative after an organisational review. |
| **No longer applicable** | The original action is no longer relevant or possible in the delegate’s circumstances. | The delegate moves to a role where the planned action cannot be carried out. |

**Developer rules:**

* The delegate selects the status for each action.

* When “Changed” or “No longer applicable” is selected, the platform asks for a brief explanation.

* When “Applied” or “Applied consistently” is selected, the platform asks for an example and when an application occurred.

* When “Completed” is selected, the platform asks what was completed and when.

* A delegate may complete a one-time action without it being used consistently. The system must retain the explanation and previous status history.

* “Started,” “In progress” and “Completed” do not automatically mean that learning has been applied. Application must be reported or evidenced separately.

* The dashboard must show the number of actions in each current status. Each action is counted once in its latest status.

* The measure they want to track.

* Why it matters.

* The baseline or starting value, if known.

* The target value, if one has been set.

* Other factors that may have influenced the result.

**14\. Measurement Rules and Data Quality**

**14.1 Core indicators**

The following are the definitions and calculations for the ITTEC 2026 pilot. The platform must show both the **number and percentage**, together with the group being measured.

| Indicator | What the platform must calculate |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Platform sign-up rate** | Number of registered delegates who created an account ÷ total valid registered delegates × 100\. |
| **Attendance rate** | Number of registered delegates recorded as attending at least part of one conference day ÷ total valid registered delegates × 100\. |
| **Pre-conference assessment response rate** | Number of delegates who submitted the pre-conference assessment ÷ number of registered delegates invited to complete it × 100\. |
| **End-of-conference assessment response rate** | Number of attendees who submitted the final assessment ÷ number of attendees invited to complete it × 100\. |
| **Transfer Plan completion rate** | Number of attendees who submitted a completed Transfer Plan ÷ number of attendees × 100\. |
| **Follow-up response rate** | Number of delegates who submitted the relevant follow-up ÷ number of delegates invited to that follow-up × 100\. Calculate separately for 30, 60, 90 and any later follow-up. |
| **Reported application rate** | Number of delegates who answered the follow-up application question and reported applying learning at least once ÷ number of delegates who answered that question × 100\. |
| **Knowledge score** | Number of correct answers to the five knowledge questions ÷ 5 × 100\. |
| **Knowledge Scores Before and After ITTEC** | For delegates who completed all five questions at both stages: average post-conference score minus average pre-conference score. Report the change in percentage points and show the number of paired delegates. |
| **Confidence change** | For each confidence statement, calculate the average post-conference rating minus the average pre-conference rating for delegates who answered that same statement at both stages. Exclude “Not applicable.” |
| **Expectation status** | Number of assessed expectations in each status ÷ total number of expectations assessed at that stage × 100\. Show each status separately. |

**Calculation example**

If **40 delegates** answer the follow-up application question and **24 report that they applied learning**, the reported application rate is:

**24 ÷ 40 × 100 \= 60%.**

The dashboard must state: **“24 of 40 respondents reported application.”** It must not state that 60% of all ITTEC attendees applied learning unless the 40 respondents represent all attendees.

**Records excluded from calculations**

The platform must exclude:

* Test records.

* Duplicate records confirmed as duplicates.

* Cancelled registrations from the valid registration count.

* Unanswered questions from the relevant question’s denominator.

* “Not applicable” responses from scored confidence calculations.

The platform must retain a record of corrections made by authorised users.

**15.2 Data interpretation**

The platform and reports must:

* Label self-reported information clearly.

* Show the number of respondents behind each percentage.

* Report knowledge, confidence and satisfaction separately.

* Distinguish intended application from actual application.

* Identify missing responses and other limitations relevant to interpretation.

**15.3 Setting Up and Managing Assessment Questions**

The development team must enter the questions provided in this workplan into the platform and organise them by stage:

1. Pre-conference assessment.

2. Session feedback.

3. Day 1 reflection and check-in.

4. Day 2 reflection and check-in.

5. Individual Transfer Plan.

6. End-of-conference evaluation and learning assessment.

7. Post-conference follow-ups.

The platform must allow M.Bryan to view and update questions for future conferences.

**Required and optional questions**

The team must configure each question as required or optional according to the instructions in the relevant section of this workplan. A delegate must be able to submit a form once all its required questions have been answered.

**Types of answers**

The platform must support the answer formats already used in this workplan, including:

* Selecting one answer.

* Selecting more than one answer.

* Rating scales.

* Short and longer written answers.

* Dates.

* Numbers.

**Questions for physical and virtual delegates**

In the end-of-conference evaluation, the platform must show the physical experience questions to physical delegates and the virtual experience questions to virtual delegates. Their attendance format should come from their delegate record.

**Knowledge questions**

The team must enter the five knowledge questions and correct answers in Section 7.5. The same questions must appear in the end-of-conference learning assessment, using the same wording, answer options and scoring.

**Changes to questions**

If a question is changed after delegates have answered it, the platform must keep a record of:

* The original wording and, where applicable, its scoring.

* The revised wording and scoring.

* Which question each delegate answered.

This will ensure that earlier responses and reports remain accurate, even if M.Bryan updates questions for a future conference.
